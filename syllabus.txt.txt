   SYNOPSIS:

This course introduces the basic electromagnetic theory. It comprises of a study of fundamental principles that govern the static and time-varying electric and magnetic fields.


   COURSE OUTCOMES (CO):

At the end of this course, the students should be able to: 
CO1	Apply the fundamental concepts of electrostatic field using Coulomb’s law and Gauss’s law.
CO2	Apply the fundamental concepts of magnetostatic field using Biot Savart’s law and Ampere’s circuital law.
CO3	Analyse the time-varying electromagnetic fields using Faraday’s law and the electromagnetic wave propagation behaviour.

 PROGRAM OUTCOMES (PO):
	   CO - PO MATRIX:

PO1	Apply knowledge of mathematics, science and engineering fundamentals to the solution of complex electrical engineering problems.
PO2	Identify, formulate, research literature and analyse complex electrical engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions.
